Addons

PegasusXI Approved Addons and Plugins

As per the Server Rules, the only permissible addons that adventurers may utilize are those listed under the Approved Addons lists (found below) for either Ashita or Windower. For the sake of transparency, we have also listed all of the addons that PegasusXI staff have reviewed and deemed prohibited. That said, unlisted addons should be considered prohibited and the use of said addons is punishable.

PegasusXI staff recognize that there may be new, unlisted addons that adventurers would like to have reviewed for potential approval. This process is outlined below.

Addon Approval Requests
If an adventurer would like to request that PegasusXI staff review an unlisted addon for potential approval, the adventurer can submit a ticket via the Addon Request Ticket found in the #support channel on our Discord. Please note that any custom addons submitted for review must be publicly hosted. Additionally, please allow up to one week (7 days) for review. Tickets regarding addons already listed as approved or prohibited will be closed.
